Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Retro Dice (package name com.reborngames.retrodice) is published by RebornGames ("we"). This policy explains what information the App processes and why.

What the App itself stores

The App has no account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, precise location, camera or microphone.

Your preferences (sound, music, vibration, motion controls, selected dice and dice type, animation speed, theme) and the list of dice you have unlocked are stored only on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your device, these preferences can be included in your own device backup.

We do not run any analytics or crash-reporting service of our own, and we do not have servers that receive data from the App.

Motion sensors

If Motion Controls are on, the App reads the accelerometer and gyroscope only while the game screen or the motion test is open, to detect a shake and move the dice. This data is processed instantly on your device and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads from Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

Google encrypts this data in transit. Where permitted, ads may be personalised; where consent is required and not given, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent and your choices

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws) the App asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices (shown when it applies to you).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings (Google / Privacy / Ads).

Rewarded videos and in-app ads

Premium dice can be unlocked by watching an optional rewarded video ad. Only the fact that a dice is unlocked is stored, on your device. The App does not sell anything and has no in-app purchases.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

Everything the App stores stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. Data collected by Google through the Mobile Ads SDK is handled under Google's policies linked above.

Security

Communication with Google's advertising services uses encrypted connections (TLS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always available in the App (Settings > Privacy Policy) and on our website.
