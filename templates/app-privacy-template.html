This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game itself does not collect personal information. It shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

[EDIT: what this app stores, e.g. progress and settings] are saved only on your device. We do not send this data to our own servers. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location, and the game has no user accounts.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob): a banner on menu screens, an optional rewarded video (for example to revive or to get bonus coins) and occasional full-screen ads between missions. No ads are shown during gameplay.

To deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may automatically collect information such as:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

This information is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and may be used to personalise ads where you have allowed it. It is handled by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Consent and your choices

  • Consent form: if you are in a region that requires it (for example the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can choose personalised or non-personalised ads.
  • Change your choice: open the game's SETTINGS → PRIVACY (shown where required) to review or change your consent at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • No internet: the game works offline. Without a connection, no ads are loaded.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to retry when the connection returns.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android ad services permissions): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Vibrate: for optional vibration feedback, which you can switch off in the game's settings.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game, which deletes it. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google's policies. You can also delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

7. Security

We do not transmit your game data. The Google Mobile Ads SDK sends its data over encrypted connections.
